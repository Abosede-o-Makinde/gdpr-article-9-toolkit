# Article 9 processing conditions

Special category data is prohibited under UK GDPR Article 9(1) unless a condition in Article 9(2) applies. This guide is the plain-English map the checker uses. It is decision-support for a review, not legal advice.

## The eight categories

Article 9(1) lists:

- racial or ethnic origin
- political opinions
- religious or philosophical beliefs
- trade union membership
- genetic data
- biometric data processed to uniquely identify someone
- data concerning health
- data concerning sex life or sexual orientation

Biometric data that is not used to identify a person is outside this list. Measuring how accurate a biometric system is belongs in the biometric research repository, not in this checker.

## The ten conditions

The activity must name one paragraph and supply the evidence that paragraph asks for.

| Paragraph | Condition | Evidence the checker looks for |
| --------- | --------- | ------------------------------ |
| 9(2)(a) | Explicit consent | Consent recorded, purpose specified, withdrawal possible |
| 9(2)(b) | Employment, social security, or social protection | Purpose fits, law or collective agreement cited, safeguards |
| 9(2)(c) | Vital interests | Life is at risk, and the person cannot consent |
| 9(2)(d) | Not-for-profit body | Aim, members or regular contact only, no outside disclosure without consent, safeguards |
| 9(2)(e) | Manifestly made public | The person themselves made the data public |
| 9(2)(f) | Legal claims | Necessary for a claim or a court, and the claim is named |
| 9(2)(g) | Substantial public interest | A Data Protection Act 2018 Schedule 1 condition is named, with the law, safeguards, and whether an appropriate policy document is in place |
| 9(2)(h) | Health or social care | Health or social care purpose, professional secrecy under Article 9(3), and a law or health-professional contract |
| 9(2)(i) | Public health | Purpose, law cited, safeguards |
| 9(2)(j) | Archiving, research, or statistics | Purpose, Article 89 safeguards, law cited |

Condition (g) does not encode every Schedule 1 paragraph. The activity names the condition it relies on. A reviewer still has to confirm that paragraph fits.

## What the bands mean

- **FAIL** means Article 9(1) still applies: no condition was claimed, or required evidence is missing or not satisfied.
- **PARTIAL** means the Article 9(2) condition is evidenced, but an Article 30 record field is missing.
- **PASS** means the claimed condition is evidenced and the Article 30 fields in this check are recorded.

A **PASS** still carries an Article 35 notice. Special category processing needs a DPIA. This tool flags that. It does not write the DPIA.

## What this tool does not decide

It does not decide that the condition is the right one for the purpose, that a retention period is long enough, or that a Schedule 1 paragraph applies. It checks whether the activity record states the evidence those rules ask for.
